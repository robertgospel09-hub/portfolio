CREATORORBIT

Income Tracking App for Content Creators

PRODUCT DESIGNER| JUN 2025

FULL CASE STUDY ON BEHANCE

OVERVIEW

CreatorOrbit brings a creator's earnings from YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok into one place. Creators link their channels, see what each platform pays, and withdraw or transfer funds, without juggling spreadsheets or guesswork.

PHASE 01

All Your Earnings in One Place

A mobile app that gives creators the financial tracking they've been missing: one balance across every platform, with withdraw and transfer a tap away.

PHASE 02

Problems & Solutions

Three core problems: income scattered across platforms, no visibility into what earns the most, and tedious manual tracking. The answers: one multi-platform dashboard, visual breakdowns by platform and income stream, and automated imports.

PHASE 03

Design Process

Followed a four-step process: empathize with creators through research, define the core problem, ideate widely, then prototype around what users actually need.

PHASE 04

Research Findings

Surveyed 23 creators across Nigeria, the USA, and the UK. 30.4% tracked income in spreadsheets, 26.1% didn't track it at all, and 91.3% were interested in a single dashboard for all their creator income.

PHASE 05

Splash & Onboarding

A bold splash and three onboarding screens that explain the value up front: connect every account, get smarter insights, and stay in control of your data. Then a short sign up and log in.

PHASE 06

Linking Channels

A guided connection flow: grant analytics access, sign in with the platform, and confirm the link. Linked accounts then show earnings per platform at a glance.

PHASE 07

Withdraw & Transfer

From the home balance, creators withdraw to a bank, card, e-wallet, or crypto, with clear success screens to confirm every move.

PHASE 08

User Flow

Mapped the full journey from onboarding and sign up through linking channels, to the dashboard where creators view linked accounts, withdraw, and track transactions.
