HOMEBRIDGE

PropTech Platform — Rental Housing for Immigrants

PRODUCT DESIGNER| AUG 2026

FULL CASE STUDY ON BEHANCE

OVERVIEW

HomeBridge is a mobile app that connects relocatees with verified, trusted housing. Newcomers to a city face deposit fraud, fake hosts, and unverified listings, so HomeBridge links them directly with background-checked landlords, verified through share codes from employers, universities, or embassies.

PHASE 01

Safe, Verified Housing in Every City

A UX/UI redesign for iOS and Android that connects relocatees with verified accommodation through systemized trust and secure identity checks.

PHASE 02

The Challenge of Moving Somewhere New

Newcomers face deposit fraud, fake hosts, and unverified listings, with no local credentials to lean on. The answer: verified housing loops that connect them directly with background-checked landlords.

PHASE 03

Secure Localization & Registration

Multilingual onboarding in English, Spanish, French, and Swahili. Share code entry sits early in sign up, so every tenant is validated by a relocator, university, or embassy before they browse.

PHASE 04

Discovering Verified Homes

Proximity-based search with filters for price, distance, bedrooms, and lifestyle preferences. Listings show travel distance to work or campus alongside verified host reviews.

PHASE 05

Secure Deposit & Verification Flows

A three-step booking flow: summary breakdown, secure processing, and safe confirmation. Deposits stay protected and identity checks are flagged before check-in.

PHASE 06

Safety-First Verify Loop

Document upload and live selfie matching confirm tenants are real people. Landlords get a dashboard to screen requests, track listings, and manage documents in real time.
