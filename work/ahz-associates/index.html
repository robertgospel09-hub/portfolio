AHZ ASSOCIATES

Study Abroad Consultancy Website & App

PRODUCT DESIGNER| JUL 2026

OVERVIEW

AHZ Associates is a study abroad consultancy that helps students navigate international education opportunities. I designed a comprehensive desktop website and mobile app covering the complete student journey from exploration to consultation booking.

PHASE 01

Homepage & Navigation

A homepage that leads with the student's goal: a clear hero with two paths, explore destinations or book a free consultation, followed by trust stats, popular destinations, the AHZ advantage, and a simple four-step process.

PHASE 02

Study Destinations Explorer

A destinations page where students filter by region and compare countries at a glance, with average tuition, partner universities, and popular programs on every card.

PHASE 03

Consultation Booking & Support

The contact page works as a booking flow: a focused inquiry form that captures destination, program, and intake, plus global office details and an FAQ that answers common questions before they're asked.
