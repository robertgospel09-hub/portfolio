AVERO

Personal Health Record App

PRODUCT DESIGNER & DEVELOPER| OCT 2026

VIEW LIVE PROTOTYPE

OVERVIEW

Avero is a personal health record app that brings a patient's scattered medical history into one secure place and makes sharing it with clinicians fast. I designed and built it end to end, from research and user flows to a working prototype live on Vercel.

PHASE 01

Connected Care, One Health Story

Set the vision: an interoperable personal health record that puts patients in control of their history and speeds up clinical sharing.

PHASE 02

The Fragmentation Problem

Patient histories sit across portals, clinic files, and PDF folders. Sharing relies on faxes and printouts, first responders work without allergy or condition data, and tests get repeated because records can't move.

PHASE 03

User Research & Personas

Studied 200+ chronically ill patients. 78% see 3 or more providers, and 65% have repeated a test because clinics couldn't transfer records. Distilled the findings into Amara, a chronic disease manager juggling three specialists.

PHASE 04

User Flow Map

Mapped onboarding from splash, sign up, OTP, and identity verification into a home hub. From there the app branches into Timeline, Lab & Rx, Profile & Security, Alerts, and a 4-step record sharing trail.

PHASE 05

Brand Design Language

Built a calm navy and teal palette with Figtree type, guided by three principles: clarity over aesthetics, trust earned daily, and WCAG 2.1 accessibility.

PHASE 06

Projected Impact

Projected outcomes: 60% fewer duplicate tests, 3x faster record sharing via QR, emergency access in 45 seconds, and end-to-end encrypted sync.
